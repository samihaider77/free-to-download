Privacy policy

Applies to the Free-to-Download desktop app and its browser extension. Last updated October 2026.

In short: Free-to-Download does not collect, store or send your personal data to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.

What the browser extension handles

  • Media addresses on the page you are viewing. While a video plays, the extension notes the addresses of video and audio streams the page loads, so it can offer them in the Download this video menu. These are kept only in your browser's temporary session storage, per tab (at most 60, for up to 30 minutes), and are deleted when the tab navigates or closes.
  • The link and title of the page you choose to download from, used to fetch the video and name the file.
  • A pairing token that lets the extension talk to the app on your own computer. It is stored in the extension's local storage.

The extension does not read or send cookies, passwords, form data, login headers or your browsing history.

Where data goes

Only to the Free-to-Download app running on your own computer (address 127.0.0.1), and only when you click to download. Nothing is sent to the developers or to any third party.

To download a file or video, the app connects directly to the website that hosts it, just as your browser would.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Access to websites (http/https) — to show the download button over videos on the pages you visit and to notice video streams as they load.
  • webRequest — to see the type and address of media responses (not their contents) so streams can be offered for download.
  • contextMenus — for “Download with Free-to-Download” on links and media.
  • storage — to keep the pairing token and the temporary list of detected media.
  • activeTab, scripting — to list links on the current page, only when you press “Find page links” in the popup.
  • nativeMessaging — to start the Free-to-Download app on your computer when it is closed and you click a download. Nothing else is exchanged.

Data retention and deletion

Detected media addresses are removed automatically as described above. Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data. The desktop app keeps your download queue and settings in a data folder in its install folder; uninstalling or deleting that folder removes them.
